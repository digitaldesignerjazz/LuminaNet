**The argument in brief:**

For decades we have searched for a dark matter particle and found nothing. We have measured an accelerating expansion and called it dark energy. And we have accepted a singularity at the origin of everything. What if all three puzzles share one root? What if the "missing mass" is not missing matter but missing geometry — the part of spacetime's structure that general relativity averages away?

Quantum foam, the idea that spacetime is granular and fluctuating at the smallest scales, has been a theoretical curiosity since Wheeler. Recent work (including Kanatchikov & Kholodnyi, 2025) suggests the foam can source both dark-matter-like and dark-energy-like effects. I extend this: the foam's residual energy density, distributed inhomogeneously, produces the observed galactic dynamics without a new particle. And as that residual density slowly decays, gravity reasserts itself — the expansion peaks and the universe begins to contract.

The endpoint is not a singularity but a bounce: the foam's granular pressure halts the collapse and reverses it, launching a new cycle. No Big Bang. No end. A breathing cosmos.
